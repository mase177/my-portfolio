UAV GCP WebGIS

Spatial decision support for ground-control-point placement, field accessibility, UAV flight-boundary screening, and survey-route planning.

Applying artificial intelligence to automatically optimize GCP placement

Plan GCPs with coverage, accessibility and terrain considered together.

The system combines GA and NSGA-II for spatial GCP selection, then uses ACO with OpenStreetMap road data to arrange field-survey visits.
