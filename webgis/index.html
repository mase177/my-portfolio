01 UAV survey planning

UAV GCP WebGIS

Ground-control-point planning for UAV surveys.

A spatial decision-support system for GCP placement, field accessibility, flight-boundary screening and survey-route planning.

  • AOI & flight boundary Draw or import a study area and review operational constraints.
  • GCP optimization Balance coverage, uniformity, edge–centre distribution, access and terrain.
  • Field route Sequence GCP/CP visits using road access and ACO routing.

02 Climate & drought

HCMC Rainfall & Drought WebGIS

CHIRPS rainfall correction and meteorological drought monitoring.

A WebGIS for Ho Chi Minh City that compares station rainfall with CHIRPS, presents XGBoost-corrected rainfall and visualizes meteorological drought during 1993–2022.

  • Daily & monthly rainfall Browse native-resolution CHIRPS rainfall before and after correction.
  • Station evaluation Compare observed rain, raw CHIRPS and corrected rainfall at 13 gauges.
  • SPI drought maps View SPI-3, SPI-6 and SPI-12 drought-frequency rasters through time.
